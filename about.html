Our Story

About hope worldwide Afghanistan

Hope worldwide Afghanistan is an international organization operating in Kabul Afghanistan for the needy communities. It is meant to privide a platform for the needy and young individuals pursuing career goals and development through offering vocational skills training courses at free of cost that hold high value in the current job market. Also, hope worldwide Afghanistan implement in disaster relief projects for the people in need like earthquake victims and winterazion related humanitarian activities.

Our learning approach combines clear instruction, hands-on projects and supportive guidance.
